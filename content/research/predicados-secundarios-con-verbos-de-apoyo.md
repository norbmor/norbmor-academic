This chapter addresses non-verbal predication structures, focusing on secondary predicates with support verbs, specifically those that involve the verb *tener* ‘to have’. Building upon previous research on possession and copulative sentences, a proposal is developed regarding the structure of these constructions in Spanish. Two main types of structures are identified: integral, which involves a comparison between individuals, and standard, which involves a comparison within the individual. A syntactic structure is proposed for each type of construction, demonstrating how this affects the possibilities for deriving copulative sentences.
